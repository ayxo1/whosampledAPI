# Music Sampling Lookup

This context describes recorded-music sampling relationships returned by the local API.

## Language

**Samples**:
The public API collection of Sample Uses attributed to a requested artist. The plural term mirrors the source site's public route; each relationship in the collection is a Sample Use.
_Avoid_: Sample lookup, sample result

**Sample Use**:
A relationship in which a Sampling Recording incorporates material from a Source Recording.
_Avoid_: Sample

**Sampling Credit**:
The attribution that connects an artist to a Sample Use, either as a credited performer of the Sampling Recording or through a producer credit.
_Avoid_: Artist ownership

**Sampling Recording**:
The recording that incorporates material from another recording.
_Avoid_: Sampling song, destination track

**Source Recording**:
The earlier recording whose material is incorporated into a Sampling Recording.
_Avoid_: Sampled song, original track
